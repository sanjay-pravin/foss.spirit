02 — REDEFINING WEALTH

Not everything valuable
can be counted.

Imagine losing what you own. Now imagine what remains when you know how to think, learn, and begin again.

◇

MATERIAL WEALTH

What you possess

Money, objects, awards, and status.

— Can be lost or spent

— May depend on circumstances

— Cannot always solve a new problem

Valuable, but not permanent.
✳

INTELLECTUAL WEALTH

What you understand

Skills, understanding, judgment, and wisdom.

✓ Travels with you everywhere

✓ Helps you adapt and solve problems

✓ Can be shared without losing it

A lasting foundation for life.

You may have to start again with nothing.
But knowledge helps you build again.
